# Shooting Polish Status

Checkpoint baseline: alpha-0.1.40. The shooting/weapon work below is local development work and has not been published as a new build yet.

- Ballistic aim/damage remains deterministic. No random spread or random damage was added.
- AK now has real learnable aim recoil in addition to cosmetic weapon kick. The first shots are mild, sustained fire climbs more, and a fixed left/right pattern replaces RNG for gameplay aim.
- Hip-fire 12-shot climb is about 8.16 degrees; ADS applies a 0.72 multiplier (about 5.88 degrees over the same 12-shot sequence).
- Releasing fire recovers remaining recoil smoothly. Mouse/stick counter-input reduces the stored recoil debt so recovery does not fight deliberate compensation.
- The same recoil model is used by offline Aim Lab/Waves and the native online client. Server hit resolution still uses the real transmitted aim ray; no client-reported damage was introduced.
- AK visual recoil retains bounded side/lift/twist variation, with rare exaggerated comic kicks; this remains cosmetic and does not add bullet spread.
- Muzzle flash is a short multi-piece burst with light, ember, and rare alternate accent colors.
- Bullets use a layered visible tracer: core, halo and pixel bullet head.
- Cosmetic tracer width, streak length and rare hot/chunky rounds vary without changing the real ray.
- World impacts create a bright flash plus short pixel debris.
- Hits create larger red/white pixel debris and stronger lethal bursts.
- Public multiplayer receives authoritative impact metadata and renders the same hit FX.
- Remote weapons show muzzle bursts and varied shot pitch.
- Remote characters receive cosmetic torso/head hit wobble.
- Room browser outer legacy dark modal was removed and now uses the shared main-menu skin.
- Aim/ADS/cover/weapon-side regressions passed after the polish.
- Public retail 2-client WSS test passed in MATCH on alpha-0.1.38 (historical published baseline).
- Local two-client native network integration passed with AK recoil v1, including authoritative combat/state consistency.
- Added a Godot 4.7 screen/depth edge-detection pass inspired by the supplied NekotoArts Borderlands-style shader. `Y` toggles it independently of `O` comic ink and `P` pixel rendering; current defaults are comic ink ON, Borderlands edges ON and pixel/texture rendering OFF.
- Borderlands edge QA verifies the fullscreen depth pass on the Compatibility renderer and a visible frame difference with the effect enabled.
- Tightened the 3D AK hold slightly toward the body and lower (`side 1.10 -> 0.90`, `height 0.80 -> 0.72`, `distance 0.98 -> 0.92`). Side-swap clearance, ADS stability, near/far aim and cover-camera tests remain passing.
- Firing recoil is now visual-only: shots do not add any CharacterBody/world velocity. Torso/head springs and held-weapon recoil still react, with a stronger visual kick on the shotgun. Authority QA explicitly verifies a shot leaves shooter world velocity unchanged.
- Confirmed bullet damage throws dense cosmetic 3D voxel blood chunks using the victim warrior primary palette (e.g. Tasko purple, Gan cyan, CrashOut red). Particle count remains roughly `20–28` on normal hits and `38–52` on lethal hits, but chunk size is now substantially larger (`~0.115–0.205` base size before lethal scaling), making the blood readable from farther away without simply increasing count.
- The local player now has a billboarded world-space health bar above the head, driven by the same authoritative/local health state as the existing HUD.
- Locomotion was rebuilt around the supplied Rayman-style pose cycle instead of planted walking: left foot front/right foot back with torso+head high, brief low/contact compression under the body, then the mirrored right-front/left-back high pose. Latest tuning raises the feet slightly more and lengthens the airborne phase: QA measures about `±2.02 m` mirrored front/back split, roughly `0.60 m` peak foot lift, about `0.41 s` air-pose duration and only `5/85` contact frames per tested direction, while the gameplay collision body stays grounded and terrain/ramp/sharp-turn tests pass.
- Gameplay jump impulse increased from `7.4` to `8.8` in both offline movement and authoritative/local-predicted online movement.
- The 3D AK now has a `25`-round magazine and `1.0 s` reload, matching the existing KW AK weapon identity. `R` reloads on keyboard; Xbox `X` / PlayStation `Square` reloads on controller; empty magazines auto-reload.
- Reload blocks firing, plays weapon reload audio, lowers/rolls the held weapon and drops a short-lived cosmetic reload prop. Ammo/reload UI is now world-space above the local player health bar instead of the bottom-right HUD.
- Online ammo, reload timing, active weapon, spread and damage are server-authoritative and included in snapshots; clients only predict local presentation and reconcile back to authority. Input protocol is now `4` / build `kw3d-proof-20260920-kar-v4`, preventing older two-weapon clients/servers from silently mixing with the KAR/spread semantics.
- Permanent ballistic rule: headshots use the exact hit CollisionShape3D region and normally multiply weapon damage by `1.5`. AK body damage is `5.0`; AK headshot damage is `7.5`; shotgun pellets use the same multiplier. KAR is the explicit sniper exception requested for balance: `50` body damage and `1000` headshot damage, so a confirmed KAR HeadRig hit kills a 100-HP opponent instantly.
- Weapon slots are now AK → Shotgun → KAR on the mouse wheel, with independent magazine/reload state. Shotgun remains `10` pellets × `5` body damage, `2` shells and `1.2 s` reload. KAR follows the legacy KW identity (`1` round, `1.4 s` reload, `0.95 s` fire interval, KAR98 SFX) and uses a long brown scoped voxel model.
- Remote online players render the authoritative AK/Shotgun/KAR selection. The final protocol-v4 two-client integration passed as `NETWORK_CASE kar_v4_network_final PASS []`, recorded authoritative KAR and shotgun shots/reloads, and stayed inside the prediction-correction budget.
- Dedicated headshot QA verifies a real HeadRig ray resolves correctly, AK torso/head hits apply `5/7.5`, KAR torso applies exactly `50`, and a real KAR HeadRig hit sets the victim dead at `0 HP`. Shotgun QA verifies 10 real pellet rays and the two-shell slot.
- Every offline target and authoritative actor now also carries a thin invisible upright `CylinderShape3D` body bridge (`0.24 m` radius) spanning the torso/neck gap but ending below the head hurtbox. The bridge is always tagged `body`, so the previous gap no longer lets shots pass through while headshots remain true HeadRig hits. Duel/authority shooting explicitly excludes the shooter’s own hurtbody from guard/shot rays.
- Enemy death rig pieces keep the strong launch/spin but now remain visible much longer: fade starts around `1.9 s` and cleanup occurs around `2.8 s` instead of the previous ~`1.5 s` total lifetime.
- Visual defaults are now the intended outline stack: comic/mesh ink ON, Borderlands screen/depth edges ON, pixel/texture pass OFF. The three toggles remain independent (`O`, `Y`, `P`). Borderlands renders below world-space UI priority, while health/ammo/name labels render at priority `126–127`; rendered QA counted `995` visible red HP-bar pixels with Borderlands enabled.
- Blood presentation remains at roughly the same chunk counts, but pieces are larger and less uniform: chunky blocks, long shards and flat fragments use randomized aspect ratios, directional/back-spray variation, independent angular velocity, per-piece gravity (`~8.8–14`) and drag. Chunks keep full size through most of their flight and only shrink near the end instead of behaving like classic constantly-fading particles.
- Weapon presentation is now fixed to the right shoulder for both offline and online authority/prediction. Q no longer swaps sides; the hold is closer to camera center, lateral bob/lag and roll are reduced, and recoil wobble recenters faster. Camera-center ballistic convergence and muzzle/cover obstruction remain unchanged: a wall or crate between chest/muzzle and the target intentionally blocks the shot so the player cannot fire through nearby cover.
- Reworked reversible voxel damage so the original character meshes/materials remain visible at all times. Damage cells are logical only: hits write bounded local cutout masks into the existing toon/pixel shaders around the real impact point, preserving each warrior palette/texture while creating visible craters. Health restoration removes those cutouts progressively. The same mask is also applied to the rig/part ink shells so holes do not fill with black outline. Collision shapes, headshot regions and server-authoritative hit registration remain unchanged.
- Voxel damage remains bounded for performance: about `433` logical cells on the local Outrage and `~429` per current enemy, at most `26%` / `96` hidden cells per actor, at most `10` cells removed by one damage event, max `32` cutout cells per source material, only `2` lightweight debris cubes spawned per hit and at most `12` debris cubes alive per actor. There are no visible damage-grid MultiMeshes and debris has no physics body/collider. After limiting shader refreshes to only hit materials, the 8-enemy / 80-hit synthetic stress probe measured ~`35 ms` total damage-event CPU time.
- Rebuilt the 3D AK presentation as a proper chunky 21-part model (stock, receiver/top cover, handguard/gas tube, barrel/muzzle, sights, grip and three-piece curved magazine) using the existing dark-navy palette on every side instead of the prior flat pixel extrusion/black top-bottom look. QA measures a `2.09 x 1.11 x 0.41` overall profile and no black material parts.
- Unified 3D audio with the existing Options mix. AK/shotgun fire and reload, enemy fire, arena/step/explosion/heal/death sounds and hit-confirm now route through the `SFX` bus; fight music routes through `Music`. Existing saved Music/SFX sliders control these buses, default SFX for new profiles is `0.7`, and weapon base fire level is now `0 dB` before the SFX-bus multiplier.
- Added soft ADS aim assist for RMB / LT on AK and Shotgun only. Targets inside a `5.5 deg` cone and within `52 m` can receive a smooth upper-torso magnet pull only with clear line-of-sight; targets outside the cone or behind cover receive no pull. Co-op only assists hostile bots and Duel assists the opposing player. KAR is explicitly excluded from aim magnetism: scoped KAR yaw/pitch are never modified by the assist layer.
- Movement tradeoff is now weapon-specific. AK and Shotgun keep normal movement while aiming (`7.5` walk and `11.0` sprint); only scoped KAR is heavily rooted at `0.6` movement speed and cannot sprint. This rule is shared by offline movement, local prediction and authority. Dedicated aim-assist QA verifies AK/Shotgun magnetism, zero KAR magnetism, and the `0.6` KAR scoped speed.
- Hip-fire now has real authoritative spray rather than cosmetic-only bloom. AK is deliberately loose at `3.25 deg` hip spread but exact at `0 deg` while ADS; shotgun opens from `9 deg` ADS to `13.5 deg` hip; KAR is `2.4 deg` hip versus `0.05 deg` scoped. The same deterministic spread function runs offline and on authority so clients cannot choose their own bullet deviation.
- `H` now performs a local Valorant-style weapon inspect on the currently held AK/Shotgun/KAR for about `1.65 s`: the model comes up toward the camera and rotates for a readable 3D view, while firing/reloading is blocked and weapon switching cancels it. Inspect is cosmetic and does not add network traffic.
- KAR ADS uses a dedicated circular sniper scope overlay: normal crosshair/held weapon hide while scoped and camera FOV moves to `27 deg` (regular ADS remains `58 deg`, hip `74 deg`). KAR scope intentionally has no soft aim assist/lock and almost roots the player at `0.6` speed. `KAR_SCOPE_INSPECT_PASS` verifies long brown model identity (`~2.8 m` visual length), scope visibility/zoom, inspect motion/fire lock and hip-vs-scoped accuracy.
- Scoped KAR now has movement-driven aim sway to make sniper shots less trivial while moving. With movement input the real scoped yaw/pitch oscillates with a bounded roughly `0.82 deg` horizontal / `0.46 deg` vertical target amplitude; when movement stops, the offset returns through a damped spring instead of snapping instantly still. The sway modifies the real aim sent online, not only the scope graphic, and remains disabled outside KAR scope. `KAR_SCOPE_INSPECT_PASS` verifies bounded moving sway, non-instant settle and eventual rest; full two-client validation passed as `NETWORK_CASE kar_scope_sway_network PASS []`.
